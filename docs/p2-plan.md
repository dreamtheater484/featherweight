# P2: editing presentations

The work plan for P2 (IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md §8, the P2 row of the milestones table). Each session reads this first, then §8, §4.3, §10 and §11 of the plan, `docs/p1-plan.md` for what P1 built, and `docs/s2-plan.md` for how the spreadsheet viewer became an editor.

P2 makes the P1 viewer an editor: slides added, duplicated, deleted, moved and hidden; text typed and formatted, with autofit, find and replace, spelling and hyperlinks; pictures, shapes, text boxes, tables and charts inserted, moved, resized, rotated and arranged; themes, backgrounds, slide size, slide numbers, footer and date; notes; copying slides between decks; and saving `.pptx` without damaging anything, with crash recovery. P3 (presenter view, editing transitions and animations, video, comments, `.ppt`/`.odp`, encryption) and release 4.0 come after.

P2 starts now on P1's code (`claude/p1-int` 4aacca6) while P1's last measurement (the real-world corpus against PowerPoint) finishes, as S4 started alongside S3's gate. P1's fixes are merged into p2-int as they land.

## Exit criteria

- **Build from scratch:** a 10-slide deck can be built from a blank presentation with Featherweight alone (title slide, bullets, a picture, a table, a chart, shapes, a theme, notes, slide numbers) by a scripted run, and it opens in PowerPoint without a repair prompt and looks as Featherweight shows it (SSIM ≥ 0.95 per slide against PowerPoint's PDF of the saved file).
- **Never damage a file (the gate):** every corpus deck (the 18 public decks, the generated ones and the 152 real-world decks), after scripted random edits (including deleting, duplicating and moving slides, changing layouts, typing, moving and deleting shapes) and a save:
  - no new validator errors,
  - passes `fwtool check`,
  - opens in PowerPoint without a repair prompt,
  - the parts nothing edited are kept as they were (round-trip store).
  - Saving without edits writes a file PowerPoint opens unchanged, slide for slide (SSIM ≥ 0.99 against the original's PDF).
- **Editing budgets (§11):** a keystroke in a text box to screen < 16 ms typical, < 50 ms worst; a slide operation (new, duplicate, delete, move) on a 50-slide deck < 50 ms to screen; saving a typical deck runs on a worker thread; undo of any operation < 50 ms.
- **Lightweight:** the exe grows by ≤ 1.4 MB in P2 (about 11.0 MB after P1; < 15 MB at 4.0). Memory with a typical deck < 150 MB. Nothing runs when idle. Opening is no slower than at P1.
- **Fuzzed:** `fuzz_pptx` gains edits (as `fuzz_xlsx` did in S2): random operations, save, reread, check; 60 s in CI, no findings.
- **Checklist:** a P2 part in `docs/TEST_CHECKLIST.md`, which Roy passes with P1's.
- **Release:** P1 and P2 are checked together; the release follows Roy's checks (3.1 or a beta, decided then).

## Decisions during P2

- **p2-corpus, 2026-11-22: PowerPoint in Remote Desktop.** p1-corpus's probe (the 18 public decks in Roy's active Remote Desktop session against the console's references: SSIM min 0.99992) settles it: PowerPoint's open checks and PDFs run in an active session, at the console or over Remote Desktop, never in a disconnected or locked one, and never while a PowerPoint of anyone else's is running. `scripts\powerpoint-open-check.ps1` stops (exit 3 or 4) and leaves the rest "unchecked" otherwise; queued runs wait for those conditions and the shared `FeatherweightNotes\quiet-pc.lock`.
- **p2-corpus: repair detection in PowerPoint** as Excel's check does it, without relying on the prompt: alerts off, a normal read-only open, then Open2007 with OpenAndRepair ("repaired" if only that opens it, "refused" if neither), a silent repair seen as other slides than the presentation part lists, and any dialog read and cancelled ("error"). `scripts\powerpoint-open-check-proof.ps1` proves it on broken decks.

- **p2-corpus: one adapter for the edit API.** The torture test and the editing budgets both go through `DeckTarget` (`tools\fwtool\DeckTorture.h`): new, duplicate, delete, move, hide, layout, typing, notes, move, resize, rotate, delete, group, ungroup, order, format, undo, redo, save. It is written against p2-model's header once it lands; until then every edit is refused (and `budgets.ps1` skips the deck editing budgets with a note).
- **p2-corpus: the public decks in the gate** are the 27 in `tests\office\presentations`, `generated`, `edge` and `show` (the broken and malicious ones are for `fwtool check` and the reader, not for editing); then the 152 screened real-world decks.
- **p2-corpus: build from scratch** plays `tests\office\build\deck.steps` through the app's script ops (`scripts\build-deck.ps1`); the presentation tab's ops it needs are listed, with their owners, in `FeatherweightNotes\p2\p2-corpus-build-ops.md` (names proposed; the pieces may rename them).
- **p2-corpus: section lists.** `fwtool check` reports a slide in two sections, a slide in none (when the deck has sections) and two sections with one id as warnings until PowerPoint's verdict on each is recorded by the proof; then the ones PowerPoint repairs become errors. A section naming a missing slide is already an error. PowerPoint 2021's verdicts (the proof, 2026-11-22, in Roy's Remote Desktop session): it repairs a slide in two sections and two sections with one id (now errors); it opens a slide in no section and a section naming a missing slide as they are (a warning, and still an error: Featherweight never writes a dangling reference).
- **p2-corpus: fuzz findings stay out of CI until fixed.** `fuzz_pptx` checks a finding reported to its owner and not fixed yet only with `FUZZ_PPTX_OPEN=1` (listed at the top of `tests\fuzz\fuzz_pptx.cpp`, each with its reproducer), so p2-int's fuzz replay test stays green; the edits made are the same either way. The owner removes the gate with the fix.
- **p2-corpus: the torture test includes the design operations** (p2-slides' `ApplyThemeDesign`, `SetShowMasterShapes`, `Editor::SetHeaderFooter`): `theme`, `master-shapes` and `header-footer` lines, a light or dark theme made like the built-in ones (fwtool doesn't link the view's theme list).
- **p2-text: a working copy per text body.** The text being edited is a detached copy of its `p:txBody`/`a:txBody` (`TextDoc`), laid out alone by P1's `TextBlock` and drawn live over the slide's bitmap, which leaves it out (a draw filter); each keystroke commits the whole body through `Editor::SetTextBodyXml(target, xml, step, typing)` (`EditTextModel`), so p2-model's API stays small and undo sees DrawingML as written. Positions are UTF-16 offsets in the resolved runs: `a:br` is one character, a field its shown text and whole. Typing is one undo step until the caret moves, editing ends, or a new line resizes an autofit shape (that keystroke is its own step with the resize). Keystroke to screen on deck-50: 0.7 ms typical, 1.7 ms worst (`fwtool bench --slide-text`).
- **p2-text: autofit.** `normAutofit` keeps PowerPoint's stored `fontScale`/`lnSpcReduction` until the text changes; then Featherweight writes the largest of PowerPoint's 14 steps (100 %/0 down to 25 %/20 %) that fits, found by halving. `spAutoFit` sets the shape's height to its text (top kept) in the same step.
- **p2-text: prompts and marks only in the editor.** "Click to add title" (or the layout's custom prompt) and the dashed outline of the text being edited are drawn in the tab, never in the show, printing or PDF. An empty placeholder takes typing in its own look; an empty paragraph shows its bullet only while the caret is in it.
- **p2-text: formatting writes what it means.** Bold, italic, underline and strike off write `b="0"`, `i="0"`, `u="none"`, `strike="noStrike"` (the placeholder may be bold); bullets on write Arial • with `marL` 285750 × (level + 1) and `indent` -285750, numbering `+mj-lt` `arabicPeriod`; off writes `buNone` with `marL` and `indent` 0. Tab and Shift+Tab at a paragraph's start change its level. Shortcuts are the documents' (plan §6.3), plus Ctrl+K.
- **p2-text: the clipboard.** Copy puts text, RTF, HTML and Featherweight's own paragraphs (DrawingML in an HTML comment after `<!--StartFragment-->`, which other apps ignore); paste takes its own, then RTF, HTML, text, merging into the look where it lands (keeping emphasis, color and links), remaking links as relationships of the slide it lands on and giving pasted fields new ids.
- **p2-text: find, spelling.** Find and Replace go through slides (shapes in z-order, groups, table cells), then notes, in the documents' find bar; Replace All is one step. Spelling checks each run in its own language (East Asian runs, fields and `noProof` left out), cached per body text. Squiggles show under every text of the slide when the deck can be edited (none in a read-only one); F7 goes to the next misspelled word through the slides' texts, round the deck (the notes pane checks its own).
- **p2-text: whose editor.** Text editing uses the deck's editor (`Deck::Editor()`, owned by p2-objects' `ModelDeck`) through `TextEditing::Attach`, with its own `OnChange` listener: the touched slides are prepared again, undo and redo are read back into the text being edited, and editing ends when slides come, go or move or the design changes. A deck the editor can't edit (`CanEdit` false) stays read-only.

**p2-model (2026-11-22):**
- Edits are part-level copy-on-write: published part DOMs never change; an edit clones the parts it touches and publishes them; undo steps keep the versions from before and swap them back. The package stays the file as opened (never changed); edits live in a part store over it (parts, relationship sets, content types), so a save writes a snapshot on a worker thread while editing goes on.
- After an edit the model reads again only what changed (the slides touched; everything after a master, layout, theme, size or notes-master change). Replaced objects live one edit longer, so pointers stay valid until the change listeners have run.
- The recovery journal records part states, not operations: replaying needs no knowledge of the operations, so a later exe replays an older journal, and each write holds only the parts changed since the last one.
- Deleting slides or shapes only removes references; the writer leaves out parts that were reachable when the file was opened and aren't now (undo needs nothing back; unused pictures and charts leave the file). Parts the file had unreachable stay.
- Deleting a slide others link to (p2-corpus's PowerPoint check: PowerPoint repairs a slide jump left without its r:id, so emptying the r:id was wrong): the a:hlinkClick, a:hlinkHover and a:hlinkMouseOver elements that jump to it are dropped wherever they are (runs, shapes, pictures, other slides, notes pages, layouts, masters), the text and shapes staying without the link, and their relationships go. Custom shows drop the slide from their list. Deleting shapes removes their animation effects (emptied time-node lists collapse up to p:timing), builds and connector ends.
- mc:AlternateContent (torture findings): the mc:Fallback is never dropped. A shape in an mc:Choice is edited where it is; the block keeps its wrapper (math needs it), its other Choices and its Fallback. What applies to the Fallback's shape too is applied there as well (the shape with the same cNvPr id): transform, fill, outline, effects and geometry between plain shapes (sp, pic, cxnSp), name, alt text and hidden, z order and deletes inside a group in the block; z order and deletes of the whole block move or remove it with every branch. Everything else (text, crop, links) leaves the Fallback as it is (for math it's usually a picture of the equation). Shapes can't be moved into or out of a group inside the block (the Fallback would lack them or have them twice). When the Fallback was chosen (no Choice understood), its content replaces the block, so the edit is what every app shows.
- Text XML can't hold (fuzz_pptx finding): wherever text comes in (typing, replacing and setting text, notes, text boxes, shape names, alt text, link addresses and tooltips), the characters XML 1.0 doesn't allow are dropped and lone surrogates become U+FFFD, so a save never writes a part that doesn't parse. Not _xHHHH_: a:t is plain xsd:string, and PowerPoint wouldn't decode it. The edit layer's UTF-8 conversion applies the same rule to anything else it writes.
- A refused text edit right after typing in the same place undoes only itself: the typing step goes back as it was. A journal's "back to the file's own" records stay the file's own through replay, so a recovered deck's save copies those parts byte for byte.
- Tables: one implementation of the grid. p2-objects' merge-aware row and column code moved into src/slide/edit/TableGrid (fwslide), which ObjectXml's four table functions call and the edit API's table operations use; TableGrid adds merging (as PowerPoint: the top left cell spans the range, the other cells' text joins it as paragraphs; a range cutting through a merged cell is refused), splitting, column widths and row heights (the frame follows the grid). Cell properties (fill, the six borders, margins, anchor) go to a:tcPr in schema order.
- Copying slides keeping the source's design: the slide's master (with its theme) and only the layouts its slides use are copied, once per paste; a master already here that is the same (its XML without the layout list, and its theme) is used again with its same layouts, so pasting twice doesn't pile up masters. New sldMasterId and sldLayoutId ids are unique above 2^31; the copied master's layout list is rebuilt from the layouts copied.
- Size budgets (coordinator, 2026-11-22): 50 KB moves from p2-text (300 to 250 KB; it was at about 213) to p2-model (300 to 350 KB; 316 KB at /O1 with the edit layer compiled for size, about 340 with its share of P1's reader). The P2 total is unchanged. One copy of the transform and group math, the edit layer's, which p2-objects' ObjectXml calls; pasting shapes between slides and decks goes through the edit layer's ShapeClip; TableOps is wired only as the table UI needs it.
- Duplicating a slide copies its charts, SmartArt, embeddings, tags and notes, shares pictures, media and its layout, leaves comments out and renews p14:creationId. Notes on a deck without a notes master: one is made as PowerPoint's default, on a copy of the slide master's theme.
- The Editor lives in the tab's ModelDeck (p2-objects' Deck::Editor()); PresentationFile (FileSession) does the tab's saving, backup and recovery.

**p2-objects (2026-11-22):**
- Objects' headless code is its own library, `fwslideobjects` (`src/slide/objects`: frames and handles, smart guides, align/distribute/order, selection over a slide's object tree, the PresentationML of objects, chart parts with their embedded workbook), shared by the tab, fwtool and the torture script. The tab's part is `ObjectTool` (`src/slide/view/ObjectTool.*`), hooked into PresentationView with one-line calls.
- Every object edit goes through the deck's editor: `Deck::Editor()` (ModelDeck makes it on first use and owns it). Format changes (fill, outline, weight, dash, shadow) read the shape with `ShapeXml`, change only that part with `fwslideobjects`, and write it back with `SetShapeXml`, one step for the selection; moves, resizes and turns are one `SetTransforms` step.
- Dragging draws two bitmaps made at the drag's start (the slide without the dragged objects, and them alone) and moves, scales or turns the second each frame; the edit is made on release. Larger than 4096 px (zoomed far in): outlines only.
- Snapping: smart guides first (slide edges and centre, other objects' edges and centres at the same level, equal spacing), else a 6-point grid; Alt turns both off. Arrow keys nudge 6 points, Ctrl+arrow 1 point.
- Inserted objects follow PowerPoint 2021: shapes with the theme's style, lines as connectors, pictures at their size (scaled to fit, centred), tables 640 points wide with 29.2-point rows in Medium Style 2 - Accent 1, charts 640 x 360 with PowerPoint's starting data and a minimal embedded workbook (Sheet1). Editing chart data replaces the embedded workbook with a minimal one (its Excel formatting isn't kept).
- Table rows and columns added copy their neighbour's cell properties and first run's look, clear the text, and grow merges they land in; a16 row and column ids get new values.
- The clipboard has a private format (the objects' XML and the parts their relationships name, charts with their workbook); pasting outside Featherweight's objects takes pictures (files, PNG, bitmaps) and text (a new text box).
- Without a cell chosen (text editing tells it), table row and column commands work at the table's end. With the caret in a cell they work at that cell (insert above/below/left/right of it, delete its row or column), through the editor's table ops; Merge Cells needs two or more cells chosen, which text editing doesn't offer yet (a range by dragging across cells is p2-text's to add; `ObjectTool::Host::tableCells` already takes a range), and Split Cells splits the merged cell with the caret.
- Presses between objects and text editing: a shape whose text body has no characters (an inserted shape, say) is the objects': it moves when dragged, and a double-click or typing puts text in it. Placeholders and text boxes take the caret even when empty, as in PowerPoint. Test runs (`--snapshot`) cut, copy and paste objects on the run's own clipboard (`util::UsePrivateClipboard`), never the user's.

**p2-slides (2026-11-22):**
- A built-in theme is applied by changing the master's theme part in place: its name, the color scheme (a:clrScheme), the heading and body Latin typefaces, the master's p:clrMap (light or dark) and the master's background; layouts and the masters' shapes, effects, East Asian and script fonts stay. Seven themes and twelve color sets are compact data in `SlideDesign.cpp`. Theme colors alone change only a:clrScheme. A theme or colors go to the masters of the slides chosen (all masters from the Design panel).
- Slide size: 16:9 (960 × 540 pt) and 4:3 (720 × 540 pt). Smaller asks PowerPoint's question (Ensure fit / Maximize); larger keeps the content's size, centered, as PowerPoint does without asking. Content is scaled uniformly and centered.
- Background "Apply to all" writes p:bg on every slide (masters and layouts untouched); a picture background is stored once (one media part, a relationship per slide). "Hide background graphics" is p:sld showMasterSp="0".
- Header & footer (`src/slide/edit/HeaderFooter.cpp`): date, footer and slide number placeholders are made on slides from their layout's (only where the layout has one, as in PowerPoint), holding a slidenum or datetimeN field (field id copied from the layout) or the text; turned off, they're removed. "Don't show on title slide" skips slides whose layout is a title layout. The date formats are shown in the placeholder's language (en-US when none).
- A new presentation is PowerPoint 2021's 16:9 blank (Office master, 11 layouts, one title slide), its theme fwdraw's default, made by a generator that lives in the tests (`NewDeckSource.cpp`); the app ships the 21 KB of bytes it makes (`NewDeckData.inc`, checked byte for byte), 29 KB less than shipping the generator (size check, 2026-11-22). Ctrl+M takes the current slide's layout; after a title slide, the master's next one (Title and Content).
- The tab has one Editor listener (PresentationSlides.cpp `OnSlideEdits`) for every edit, undo and redo, whoever made it: thumbnails and the Deck's caches follow slides by id (`Deck::Reorder`), only touched slides are drawn again (all after a theme, master or size change), the current slide and the selection follow their slides. A slide op on a 50-slide deck takes 2–4 ms to screen (`timecmd`).
- Notes are typed in the notes pane in a native multi-line edit (IME, undo, clipboard as in Windows), plain text until p2-text's editor takes the notes; the text goes to the slide after a 1.5 s pause and on leaving. App shortcuts still work from it.
- Copying slides between tabs: the source deck is saved to memory at copy time (so closing or changing it later doesn't matter) with the slides chosen; Ctrl+V or the thumbnails' paste options insert them after the current slide through `InsertSlides`, in the destination's design by default. Other programs still get the slide as a picture and its text.
- The read-only banner and status show only when the edit layer can't edit the deck (with its reason); macros: "kept but never run".
